📊 Volume + MA + RSI

Complete Trading System | 3 Powerful Indicators | Tamil Guide

🎯 "Less is More - 3 Indicators are Enough!"
📊
VOLUME

Volume = Truth Confirmation

  • 📈 Price + High Volume = Strong Move ✅
  • 📉 Price + Low Volume = Weak Move ❌
  • 📊 Volume > 20 MA Volume = Active Trading
  • ⚡ Volume Surge = Big Move Coming
✅ Buy Signal: Price Up + Volume Up
❌ Sell Signal: Price Down + Volume Up
📈
MOVING AVERAGE (MA)

MA = Trend Direction

  • 🟢 Price > 20 MA = Short Term Uptrend
  • 🟡 Price > 50 MA = Medium Term Uptrend
  • 🔴 Price > 200 MA = Long Term Uptrend
  • ✨ Golden Cross = 20 MA above 50 MA
  • 💀 Death Cross = 20 MA below 50 MA
✅ Buy Signal: Price above 20,50,200 MA
❌ Sell Signal: Price below 20,50,200 MA
🎯
RSI (Relative Strength Index)

RSI = Entry Timing

  • 🟢 RSI < 30 = Oversold → BUY Zone
  • 🟡 RSI 30-50 = Accumulation → Watch
  • 🟠 RSI 50-70 = Distribution → Caution
  • 🔴 RSI > 70 = Overbought → SELL Zone
✅ Buy Signal: RSI below 30 (Oversold)
❌ Sell Signal: RSI above 70 (Overbought)
🎯 3 Indicators Combo - Complete Strategy

🟢 STRONG BUY

✅ Price > 20 MA
✅ RSI 30-50
✅ Volume > Average
⚡ Action: BUY with confidence

🔴 STRONG SELL

✅ Price < 20 MA
✅ RSI 50-70
✅ Volume > Average
⚡ Action: SELL / Exit longs

🟡 NO TRADE ZONE

❌ Price sideways
❌ RSI 50-60 (Neutral)
❌ Low Volume
⚡ Action: WAIT for clear signal
